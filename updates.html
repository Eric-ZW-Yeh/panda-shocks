Planned

  • v1.1 (2027). Add 17 dated announcements that are already in the working database but not in v1.0: 14 failed-pregnancy outcomes and 3 pregnancy announcements. They affect no birth-based series. Each Google Trends pull will be repeated three times on different days and averaged; v1.0 uses single pulls.
  • Annual updates, each first quarter. Extend the panel by one calendar year: new births, namings, deaths, arrivals and returns; new hard political events; changes in visa and travel regimes. Each update comes with a fresh Google pull for the added year. Doses of earlier events are recomputed only if Google revises its series, and any revision is listed here.
  • Coverage. A new host jurisdiction enters the panel when a loan to it begins.

Versioning

Each release is archived under its own DOI. The concept DOI always resolves to the latest version. Files keep their names and column order within a major version. A change to a definition raises the major version and is documented in CHANGELOG.md.
