Checksums are also in SHA256SUMS.txt. To verify a file, run sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt inside data/, or use certutil -hashfile FILE SHA256 on Windows.

Which series to use

  • z_birth_nat, the SPS (national dose), is the headline series. Start the sample in 2011-01, as the paper does.
  • z_birth_reg (the SPS, regional dose), z_birth_count (the SPS, count) and z_birth_cap90 (the SPS, capped dose) are robustness versions: regional dose, no dose, and the national dose capped at its 90th percentile.
  • z_death is the negative biological counterpart. z_hard, n_conflict and n_summit describe hard political events, which governments choose and which are therefore not exogenous.
  • A_panda (attention to the panda, monthly Google index) and A_china are Google's monthly indices, to be used as the endogenous variable or as a first-stage check.

Outcome data

Trade, tourism and co-authorship series are not redistributed because they are covered by third-party licences. download_outcomes.md lists the sources (IMF IMTS, UN Comtrade, Eurostat Comext and tourism tables, national tourism statistics, OpenAlex) and what to download from each.
